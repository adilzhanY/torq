1 600 icons, ISC licence, 2 px rounded strokes. It is a good pack, the reason to look elsewhere is not quality, it is that lucide is everywhere, and torq has spent this week buying its own geometry: the eight-blade vortex, the shield badges, a hand-drawn streak mark. A rounded-terminal outline set is the least torq-like thing left on the screen.

Where it sits wrong

  • Round terminals and round joins against a SHARP-10 radius system and a logo made of blades.
  • Outline only. No filled weight, so an active tab can only change colour, the dock's selected state has to be carried entirely by the lime and the rail.
  • The dumbbell is its most recognisable glyph and it reads as two blobs at 21 px.

Sharper corners, same weight. The safe upgrade.

Pack 1

Recommended5 900 iconsMIT

Tabler

The closest thing to a sharper lucide. Same 2 px outline language so nothing else in the app has to change, but the corners are squarer and the geometry more constructed. It looks drawn on a grid, which is exactly what SHARP-10 and the blade logo are doing.

Why it fits torq

  • Drop-in. Same stroke width, same 24-unit box, same outline idiom, so the swap is a rename in Icon.tsx, not a redesign.
  • 5 900 icons, nearly four times lucide, so nothing you reach for later is missing.
  • Squarer terminals sit with the sharp radii instead of arguing with them.
  • Official @tabler/icons-react-native; MIT.

What it costs

  • It is still an outline set. If you want a filled active tab you have to reach for the separate -filled variants, which exist for only part of the library.
  • The change is subtle, from a metre away this dock and the current one look alike.
  • Its barbell is more literal than lucide's, which some will read as clip-art.

Pack 2

Boldest6 weightsMIT

Phosphor

Six weights of the same 1 512 glyphs, and the one that matters here is Fill. The active tab in this dock is not just lime. It is solid, which is the pattern the app already adopted when the streak mark went from a stroked flame to a filled one.

Why it fits torq

  • Fill on the active tab, the strongest selected state of the three, and it matches the solid streak mark and solid rank shields.
  • Six weights (thin → bold → fill → duotone) means one family covers every density you will ever need without mixing packs.
  • Geometric, slightly severe, with a distinct personality. Nobody will mistake it for the default.
  • Official phosphor-react-native; MIT.

What it costs

  • 1 512 unique glyphs, fewer than lucide's 1 600. With six weights it is 9 000 files, but if you count distinct concepts, this is the only option here that does not beat what you have. Worth checking the few you rely on before committing.
  • Two weights in the dock means two icon lookups per slot and a slightly heavier component.
  • Its house and medal are noticeably rounder than Tabler's, less angular than the brand.

Line idle, fill active. Note the workout glyph.

Pack 3

Softest3 058 iconsApache 2.0

Remix Icon

Every glyph ships as a matched line and fill pair, designed for exactly this: outline when idle, solid when selected. Rounder and friendlier than the other two, so the question is whether torq wants friendly.

Why it fits torq

  • Line/fill pairs across the whole set, not just part of it, so the filled active state never has a gap.
  • 3 058 icons, nearly double lucide.
  • Heavier optical weight than lucide, which holds up better on a near-black page.
  • Apache 2.0, the most permissive licence here for a paid app.

What it costs

  • Roundest of the three. On a page built from blades and 10-px corners it softens the brand rather than reinforcing it.
  • No dumbbell. The nearest workout glyph is a boxing glove, which is not what the tab does.
  • No official React Native package. You would render the SVGs yourself or add a wrapper.

My read: Tabler if you want the app to look more like itself with one afternoon's work. It is the same idiom with the corners the brand actually wants, and four times the library, and the migration is a rename map behind the Icon component you already have. Phosphor if you want the dock to gain something lucide structurally cannot give it: a filled active tab, which is the same move the streak mark just made. Remix I would not ship, the roundness pulls against the brand, there is no official RN package, and its workout tab would be a boxing glove.

One thing none of these change: the icons are not the reason the app looks generic. The vortex, the shields and the streak mark are already yours. This is the last 10%.
