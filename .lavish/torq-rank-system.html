The full picture as it stands after the 2026-08-04 planning round (locked in PATH.md) and the badge design sessions. Every lift answers "how good am I, compared to whom?", normalized to your body, backed by real data, framed by world records, shared with friends.

1 · How a set becomes a rank

Pure TypeScript over bundled JSON: offline-first, no server round-trip. The big barbell lifts get real percentiles (OpenPowerlifting dump, sex × weight class); ~50 popular accessories get a calibrated DOTS-style formula; the long tail stays PR-only (unranked) at first.

Sex + bodyweight only

The only inputs to the math, the science-backed pair every points formula uses. Height and age show on the profile but stay out of the formula (no accepted formula exists; masters coefficients are a later open question).

e1RM rules

Epley estimate is rank-eligible only from sets of ≤10 reps (it degrades beyond that). Warmups never count, consistent with the existing PR engine in stats.ts.

Honest framing

OpenPowerlifting samples competitive lifters, so the claim reads "top 18% of competitive lifters", honest and more impressive-sounding.

2 · The ladder, 9 tiers × 4 stages

The shield + orbit system from the last design round, with a new emblem replacing the mountain: one orbit ring that grows with the stage, I bare · II ring · III ball · IV two balls, and the laurel wreath reserved for World Class, which also gets the cycling holographic frame. Elite wears the brand lime. Percentile bands below are calibration placeholders to tune against the real data.

5 · Anti-cheat & where we are

The Liftoff lesson

  • Friends-first visibility, your friends know you don't bench 300 kg; self-policing, zero moderation
  • Plausibility caps (claims above the world record for your class get flagged / unranked
  • Video-verified badge) later, for top-of-ladder spots; global leaderboards only in Phase 4 with this stack in place
