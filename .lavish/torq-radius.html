torq · design decision

How round should torq be?

We went cardless, dark, and Space Grotesk — but every chip, button, field and dock capsule is still fully rounded (R.pill = 999) or very soft (R.md = 22). That radius is left over from the warm clay/bento skin. Here is what it looks like at four settings, and what I think we should lock in.

My take

Yes — go less round. Land on 10 px for controls, 8 px for chips and fields, and keep the pill for one job only: status labels (LIVE, tier pills, PR badges).

The logo is sharp now

The vortex is eight pointed blades. A capsule-shaped button under a bladed mark reads as two different brands. 10 px keeps the app friendly but stops fighting the logo.

Cardless needs edges

With no cards, the only shapes left on screen are the interactive ones. Fully-round shapes look like floating candy on a bare black page; a tighter radius reads as a deliberate control.

Pills stop meaning anything

When buttons, tabs, fields and badges are all pills, "pill" carries no information. Reserve it for status and the shape starts communicating again.

Not fully square

0–4 px reads as brutalist/dev-tool — wrong for a gym app people should find pretty. 10 px is the line where it stays premium (Apple Fitness, Whoop, Strava all sit 8–14 px).

What stays fully round — non-negotiable

Circles that are circles

Week-strip dots, avatar, rank badge orbit balls, the quick-start play chip. A circle is not a rounded rectangle.

Progress bars

The 5 px rank/goal bars keep round caps — a squared 5 px bar looks broken, not sharp.

Status pills

LIVE, tier labels, PR badges, streak flame. This is the one place the pill earns its shape.

The rank shields

The hex badge geometry is its own system and is already sharp. Untouched.

Cost

Small. R is one object and 48 usages already reference it. The real work is auditing the ~31 hardcoded 999/99 radii scattered in components and deciding per case whether each is a true pill or a leftover — roughly an hour, screen by screen, no risk of behavior change.
