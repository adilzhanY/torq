Three streak marks

All three are drawn from scratch in the app's own geometry — tapered blades, sharp tips, one continuous silhouette — the same language as the vortex logo. Every shape below was rendered at 14 px and checked before it got here, because the streak mark lives in a pill next to a number and that is where icons die.

Concept A

RecommendedSafest

Cut flame

A solid flame with two blades cut out of it, curving the way the logo's blades curve. The silhouette is a flame at any size, so it never stops being readable; the vortex only shows up once the mark is big enough to reward looking at.

What it gets right

  • Unmistakably fire at 14 px, which is the size that actually ships.
  • Solid, not stroked — it matches the filled marks the rest of the dark UI uses.
  • The cuts read as the logo's rotation, so it belongs to torq without shouting about it.
  • Survives the lime pill, where a dark-on-lime mark loses all its thin detail.

What it risks

  • The most conservative of the three. At a glance it is still 'a flame' — the ownership is in the detail, not the silhouette.
  • The slits close up below ~14 px, where it degrades to a plain flame.

Concept B

Sharpest

Blade fan

The same flame built from three separate blades with air between them. Nothing is cut out; the negative space between the tongues does the work, which makes it the most obviously hand-drawn of the three and the closest to the eight-blade mark.

What it gets right

  • Most distinctive silhouette. Nobody else's streak icon looks like this.
  • The sharp tips are exactly the SHARP-10 language the rest of the app moved to.
  • Scales up beautifully — this is the one that would look best on the streak dialog.

What it risks

  • Three thin tongues at 14 px is the riskiest thing here; the gaps start to fill in and it can read as noise.
  • Its earlier version read as a crown, not a flame — the shape is one bad tweak away from the wrong noun.
  • On the lime pill the thin dark tongues lose weight fastest.

Concept C

Boldest

Torque tongue

One flame that curls, thick at the root and razor at the tip, with a loose ember in the eye of the curl. It is the only one that says something about the app's name: fire, but spun. A streak with rotation in it.

What it gets right

  • The most ownable mark of the three — it is a flame nobody would mistake for a stock icon.
  • The curl gives it an obvious animation: the ember orbits the eye, matching the rank badge.
  • Reads as motion even when static, which is what a streak actually is.

What it risks

  • It reads as fire second and as a comma first for some people; that is the bet.
  • The ember becomes a dot at 14 px and can look like a printing flaw.
  • Furthest from the universal streak language — the one that needs the count beside it to be understood.

My read: A is the one I would ship and C is the one I would want to. A is a flame at every size a user will ever see it, and its ownership is real but quiet — the safe answer for a mark that appears at 14 px next to a number. C is genuinely yours, animates beautifully, and is the only one that argues for the app's name; the price is that it needs its count beside it before it reads as a streak, which it always has. B is the sharpest silhouette and the weakest at small size — I would use it only if the streak mark got bigger everywhere. Whichever wins, the hand-drawn Lottie flame in the streak dialog gets redrawn to match, or the two will fight.
