The live session, three ways

You like the shape of the screen, so nothing here throws the set list away. The question is how much to change: tighten what exists, focus on one exercise at a time, or move logging into a bottom bar you can reach with a thumb. Pick one and I build it.

Where we are

What the screen does today

Real screenshot, not a mock. Five things on it are costing more than they return.

  1. 1
    “2:00” is printed five times per exercise The rest divider repeats between every set, and it is the same number every time. Twenty-five of them in a five-exercise session, all saying nothing new, but they own a whole strip of vertical space each, which is why the rows sit so far apart.
  2. 2
    PREVIOUS repeats itself and eats a third of the width “70 kg × 8” three times, then two em-dashes. It is the widest column on the page and the one you read least.
  3. 3
    The check is the smallest thing on screen It is the most-tapped control in the app, a grey ghost box at the far edge, with a thumb travelling the full width of the phone to reach it.
  4. 4
    Nothing says which set you are on Every row looks identical until you tick it. Coming back from a rest you have to re-find your place.
  5. 5
    The header counts volume “0 sets · 0 volume”. Volume was cut from Home, Stats and History because you said nobody measures it. This is the last place it survives, and the header has room for something you actually want mid-session.

Last time greys in behind the field. Rest lives once, in the header.
  • The 25 repeated “2:00” strips become one tappable rest chip per exercise, rows sit together, the list gets roughly a third shorter.
  • PREVIOUS moves inside the empty field as ghost text (70 × 8), so last time is where you type, not in a column beside it. It disappears the moment you enter something.

  • The header counts sets and draws a thin progress line. Volume is gone.
  • Still a long scroll on a six-exercise day.
  • Smallest change. It fixes the screen, it doesn't reinvent it.

Nothing below the fold. The whole lift fits.
  • No scrolling mid-lift, the exercise, its history, its sets and the rest bar all fit one screen.
  • The rail across the top is a live map of the session: done lifts fill lime, you can tap ahead or back.
  • Room to be generous: bigger fields, the suggestion spelled out in words instead of a tiny arrow.
  • Rest is a real bar under the sets, not a strip wedged between rows.
  • Supersets and “add a bit of everything” days get more tapping, you move between lifts explicitly instead of scrolling.
  • Biggest rebuild of the three, and the most different from every other gym app.

After logging, the same tray becomes the rest countdown and then loads set 4.
  • Everything you touch sits in the bottom third, reachable one-handed, mid-set, with chalk on.
  • Steppers instead of a keyboard: no numeric pad covering the screen, no mis-taps on 725 instead of 72.5.
  • The tray tells you the plan out loud (“set 3 · +2.5 suggested”) instead of a 10 px arrow.
  • Rest takes over the same bar, so the timer is always in the same place.
  • Editing an old set means tapping it to load it back into the bar, one extra step.
  • Steppers are slower for a wild jump (40 → 100 kg); needs a tap-the-number keypad escape hatch.

Regardless of which

Small fixes worth taking anyway

These are independent of the direction. Tick the ones you want in the form below.

ChangeWhat it fixes
Header counts sets, not volume “3 of 17 sets” with a thin progress line. Volume was cut everywhere else in the app; this is the last screen showing it.
Spell out the suggestion The tiny green arrow on the KG field means “+2.5 from last time” and nothing on screen says so. One line of text under the exercise name does.
One rest chip per exercise Replaces the repeated “2:00” strips. Tap it to change every set's rest at once, which is already what the ⋯ menu's “Update rest timers” does, just buried.
Sticky exercise name while you scroll On a 5-set lift the name scrolls off before you finish it, so you can lose which lift the fields belong to.
Finish button always reachable Right now it sits at the bottom of the whole list; you scroll past five exercises to end a session.
Live PR flag on the row If the set you just ticked beat your best, say so then, not only in the summary after you finish.

Your call

Pick a direction

My recommendation is A: you said you like the overall design and want small changes, and A is exactly that, it deletes the noise and promotes the check without asking you to learn anything. B and C are both real improvements, but they change how the screen is operated, which is a bigger bet than the problems justify right now.
