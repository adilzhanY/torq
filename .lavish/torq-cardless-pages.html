Cardless — every page, type does the work

New design principle, now in CLAUDE.md: no more card containers for content. Text sits directly on the page background; hierarchy comes from type scale, weight, color and whitespace; hairlines separate where needed. Surfaces survive only where you touch them — buttons, inputs, the dock — and in true overlays. Below: all seven pages rebuilt on that rule, in the rebrand skin (near-black · lime · Space Grotesk).

1 · The rule

What dies

Card backgrounds, box shadows, rounded containers around plain content, "bento" grids of boxes, chart frames. Every pixel of chrome that isn't information.

What replaces it

Type scale (26/17/13/10), weight (700 vs 400), color steps (ink → dim → faint), eyebrow labels, hairline dividers, and generous whitespace. Numbers get big; labels get small.

What survives

Interactive surfaces only: the lime CTA pill, inputs, the dock, the top bar — plus real overlays (dialogs, sheets). Charts keep their plots, lose their frames.

2 · Same content, boxes vs type

The Home hero as it ships today (clay cards) against the cardless rebuild. Nothing was removed — only the chrome.
