Rank badges, 4 visual concepts

Nine tiers: Rust → Iron → Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond → Elite → World Class. Every concept is pure SVG (ships as components, no image assets), built on the locked rebrand: sharp tau, lime #C8FE23 on near-black. Two deliberate color rules across all concepts: Elite is the brand-lime tier (reaching the brand color = reaching the top of the human ladder), and World Class goes holographic (animated iridescence, the only tier that cycles color).

A

Forged Crest

An angular kite-shield forged from the tier's metal, tau embossed dead-center. The facet lines make it read as machined metal, not a flat sticker. Tier is encoded twice: the metal gradient and the pip count on the lower facet (1–9), so colorblind users still read rank. Animation: a specular shimmer sweep across the metal + a breathing rim glow on high tiers. The most "game rank" of the four: strongest for share cards and rank-up moments.

B

Torque Ring

A dial of 9 arc segments around the tau: your tier is how many segments are lit, in the tier metal. This is the same visual language as the app's existing ArcGauges on Home, so it feels native on day one, and it's the only concept where progress to the next tier is drawable: the next segment can fill partially (see the hero, Diamond at 60% toward Elite). Animation: segments ignite sequentially on mount, the partial segment breathes, tau glows.

C

Cut Gem

The badge is a gemstone and the cut itself levels up: Rust–Bronze are a rough 6-facet cut, Silver–Platinum an 8-facet cut, Diamond and Elite a 10-facet brilliant, and World Class a 12-point star. Higher rank literally means more light. Tau sits engraved in the table facet. Animation: a rotating specular flare sliver sweeping the facets, like light moving over a stone. The silhouette change means you can tell Gold from Diamond at a glance even in grayscale.

D

Plate

The quiet one: the app icon's rounded dark square, tau struck in the tier metal, a thin tier-metal ring, and a 9-slot notch rail along the bottom edge (lit notches = tier). Background stays near-black for every tier, so a column of these in a list stays calm, only the tau's metal changes. Animation: a vertical scanline pass + the newest notch blinking. This is the concept that looks best inside the UI; the crest looks best outside it.

A pairing, not a single pick

These jobs pull in opposite directions: a badge living in a 24 px exercise row wants to be quiet; a rank-up share card wants a trophy. My lean: Torque Ring (B) as the system badge, it inherits the ArcGauge language you already ship, and it's the only one that can show "820 pts to Elite" in the badge, paired with Forged Crest (A) as the celebration/share asset (rank-up modal, share cards, profile hero). Same tier metals, same tau, two silhouettes for two jobs. Cut Gem's shape-escalation trick (silhouette changes per tier band) is worth stealing into whichever direction wins.
